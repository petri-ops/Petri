## Loading rule

1. Always load `01-foundations.md` first if the question involves perception, grouping, or hierarchy.
2. Load the primary discipline reference(s) from the table.
3. Always finish with `08-ui-translation.md` and `09-identity-test.md` when the output affects an interface.

## Source provenance

Discipline principles below are synthesized from standard art/design education: Bauhaus/Itten color theory, Gestalt perception, Xie He's 六法 (`古画品录`), Swiss International Style, classical film composition and continuity, and general architecture theory. Web anchors consulted during creation: creativehut.org (color schemes), gestaltprinciples.com / toptal.com (Gestalt in UI), 新华网/文汇报 (谢赫六法), Northwestern Open Books (visual design in UCD), abstractopedia.org (composition). These are background literacy, not copied assets.
